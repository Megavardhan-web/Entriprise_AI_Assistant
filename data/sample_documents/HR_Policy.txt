SECTION 1: ANNUAL LEAVE

Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave must be requested through the NexaCore Employee Portal at least
5 working days before the intended leave date.

Leave requests require approval from the employee's direct manager.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to 5 days into the next calendar
year. Carried-forward leave must be used by 31 March.

SECTION 2: SICK LEAVE

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

A medical certificate is required for sick leave lasting more than 2 consecutive
working days.

Employees should inform their manager before 10:00 AM on the first day of
absence whenever possible.

SECTION 3: WORKING HOURS

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees receive a one-hour lunch break.

SECTION 4: REMOTE WORK

Employees may request up to 2 remote-working days per week.

Remote work requires manager approval and must be recorded in the Employee Portal.
