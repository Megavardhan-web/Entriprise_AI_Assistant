NexaCore Technologies — Procurement SOP

Document ID: PROC-007

Version: 1.0

Effective Date: 1 August 2026

Status: Current

Department: Procurement

Owner: Procurement Operations

1. Purchase Request

Employees must submit purchase requests through the Procurement Portal.

Each request must include a business justification, estimated cost, required delivery date, and suggested vendor if known.

2. Approval Thresholds

Purchase Value Required Approval
Up to ₹25,000 Department Manager
₹25,001 to ₹2,00,000 Department Manager and Procurement Manager
Above ₹2,00,000 Department Manager, Procurement Manager, and Finance Director

3. Vendor Evaluation

For purchases above ₹50,000, procurement should obtain at least three vendor quotations unless an approved exception applies.

Vendors should be evaluated on price, quality, delivery timeline, warranty, and compliance requirements.

4. Purchase Order

A purchase order must be issued before the vendor begins work or ships goods, except for approved emergency procurement.

5. Receiving Goods

The requesting department must verify the quantity and condition of received goods before confirming receipt in the Procurement Portal.

6. Invoice Matching

Finance verifies the invoice against the purchase order and goods receipt before processing payment.

7. Emergency Procurement

Emergency purchases require written approval from the Procurement Manager. The purchase request must be documented within 2 working days.

8. Contact

Questions should be sent to procurement@nexacore.example
